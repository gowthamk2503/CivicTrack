About This Platform

Our Mission

The Road Maintenance Portal connects citizens with local authorities to improve road safety and maintenance. By providing an easy way to report issues and track their resolution, we help create safer communities.

Key Features

  • Easy Reporting: Submit road issues with photos, descriptions, and location information.
  • Real-time Tracking: Monitor the status of your reports as they progress from Pending to In Progress to Resolved.
  • Admin Dashboard: Authorities can efficiently manage and prioritize maintenance work.
  • Secure Authentication: Password-protected accounts with industry-standard security.
  • Statistics: View detailed analytics on issues and resolutions.

How It Works

  1. Register: Create an account as a citizen.
  2. Report: Submit details about road issues you've found.
  3. Track: Monitor your report's progress with real-time updates.
  4. Resolve: See your issue fixed and marked as resolved.
